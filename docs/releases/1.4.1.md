## Highlights

- **The Quick Note dock closes when you are done with it.** Click anything else — another window, the document behind it — and the panel goes back to being a tab. It is always on top, absent from the taskbar and absent from alt-tab, so nothing else about the desktop told you it was still open.
- **Its close button works.** The panel's header is a drag surface, and a drag holds on to the pointer in a way that swallowed the click that ended on Close. The two controls in the header are now let past it.
- **The dock goes where you put it.** Taking hold of the panel by its header used to throw it a quarter of a screen downwards, and the tab jumped by half its height, because both moved their middle to the cursor rather than the part you had grabbed.

## Improvements

- A press on the tab opens the panel however long you hold it. The desktop dock told a click from a drag by duration — anything over 220ms was a drag — so a deliberate press opened nothing at all. It now asks the one part of Noto that can actually see the cursor whether the dock moved.
- A press that goes nowhere leaves the dock exactly where it was. Every press used to start the window following the cursor, and every one of them wrote a new placement to disk.
- Dragging behaves the same on either edge and across displays, including while the panel is open.
- The Smart Sidebar's rail closes on a click outside it too.

## Bug fixes

- The dock panel could not be dismissed by clicking away from it, on the desktop or in the application window.
- Close and the move-to-the-other-edge button did nothing when the panel was dragged from the desktop's dock window.
- Escape, the close button and a click elsewhere now share one path out of the panel, so the window's size and what it is drawing can no longer disagree.
